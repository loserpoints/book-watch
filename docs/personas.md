# Personas

Who the app is built for. Each job in [jobs](jobs.md) links the personas it serves, and a job can serve more than one.

## P1 · Reader

### Who

A reader who borrows from the library first and, when it doesn't have a book, buys a cheap used copy to read and then donate.

### How they buy

- The library comes first. Buying is for the books it doesn't have.
- A copy is read and passed on, so it has to be cheap and readable, not a particular edition.
- The delivered price against the book's limit decides most purchases.
- Copies are ruled out at a glance, by price, the limit and condition, rather than one listing at a time.
