# Bought

## Purpose

Bought keeps a record of every book I've bought through the app, a ledger by month, with what I paid and where.

## What it shows

- The top bar, with Bought as the current tab (see [want list](want-list.md#what-it-shows)).
- One section per month, newest first, headed by the month in the title face, "September 2026", with how many books and what they cost at the right, "3 books · $39.60", over a rule. The cost is left out of a month paid in more than one currency.
- One line per book bought, most recent first, with no rule between books: its small cover, its title and author, and at the right what was paid delivered, then the day, "Sep 21", then where, "AbeBooks" or the shop's name.
- What was paid is green with ✓ at or under the limit the book had when it was bought, and red over it (see [pricing](../rules/pricing.md)). A book that had no limit shows its price plain.
- The price, day and where sit in a column no wider than it needs, up to a limit, and a long shop name wraps inside it. For eBay and AbeBooks they are no taller than the cover, so the cover sets each line's height.
- A small cover with no image shows a plain board, since the title is beside it.
- "Nothing bought yet. A book marked bought on the want list shows here." before anything is bought.

## What you can do

- Mark a book bought from the want list's trash, and it appears here (see [want list](want-list.md#what-you-can-do)). A bought book can't be put back.
- Nothing on a line is a link: the book is off the list.
- Tap Wanted in the top bar to go to the want list. Tap Bought while here to go back to the top.
- Press back to return to the want list.
