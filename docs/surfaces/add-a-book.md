# Add a book

## Purpose

Adding a book puts it on the want list, by searching for its title or by entering its ISBN.

## What it shows

- A sheet opened by the + button, with a switch between Title & author and ISBN.
- On Title & author: "A title is enough. Add the author to narrow it down.", a title field and an optional author field.
- The search results, each with its cover, title, author, year and number of editions.
- "On your list" in place of Add on a result already on the list.
- "Not here? Switch to ISBN." below the results.
- On ISBN: "An exact edition, by its number." and an ISBN field.
- An error, in the sheet, when an ISBN's check digit is wrong, Open Library has no record of it, Open Library cannot be reached, nothing matches the title, or the book is already on the list.
- An "Add anyway" option after an ISBN error, which searches eBay for exactly what was typed.

## What you can do

- Tap + to open the sheet.
- Search by title, with or without the author.
- Tap anywhere on a result to add it. The sheet closes and the new book starts checking on the want list.
- Switch to ISBN and add a book by its number.
- Add an ISBN or text anyway after an error.
