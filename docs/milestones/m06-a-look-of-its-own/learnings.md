# M6 · A look of its own

## Learnings

- Reviewing each screen as a user found defects the suite passed, including over never shown and a false ISBN warning. [CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#reviewing)
- Design was settled by looking at real screens at phone width, and the copy row took five rounds where a stopping rule would have taken two. [CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#building)
- Every slice was rendered and looked at, which caught a dropped typeface and clipped titles. [CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#testing)
- Open Library asks for covers to be displayed from its server, not downloaded. [Rate limits](../../rules/rate-limits.md#open-library)
- A book Open Library was never asked about was recorded as having no cover. Not asking is not an answer. [Matching](../../rules/matching.md#grading)
