# M4 · What I will pay, and whether this is fair

## Learnings

- eBay offers no sale data, so every price is an asking price, and the wording is where that gets lost. [Pricing](../../rules/pricing.md#prices)
- New and used are two markets, not two grades. [Pricing](../../rules/pricing.md#markets-and-ranks)
- Every price is a delivered price, because shipping is where a seller can hide margin from a sort. [Pricing](../../rules/pricing.md#prices)
- An overseas listing displaces a US one rather than adding a row, so the US filter belongs in the search. [Matching](../../rules/matching.md#searching)
- Breaking the code on purpose found two tests that could not fail. [CONTRIBUTING](../../../CONTRIBUTING.md#testing)
