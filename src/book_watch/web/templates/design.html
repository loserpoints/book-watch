Book rows

Every state a book on the want-list can be in. A caret after the price, ▾ or ▴ in its color, says which way the latest check moved it: tap it for what it was, and tap anywhere to close it. Checked with a price under, over and with no limit; a long title; maybes; checking; couldn't check; not checked yet; nothing listed; still digging; throttled. Tap anywhere on a row to open its book. The trash, "digging" and "Throttled" keep their own taps.
